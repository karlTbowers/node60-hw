Busbars

SurLok stud length is not confirmed. The busbar sits between each receptacle and its REDCUBE terminal. With the library's estimated 7.7 mm stud, about 4.5 mm reaches the terminal's 5.6 mm thread. Get the real stud length from Amphenol before release.
Header pin tails on 2.6 mm. AT13 tails are 3.1 ± 0.4 mm and ATM13 tails 3.05 mm, leaving about 0.1–0.5 mm of protrusion. Confirm the joints with Amphenol or on a first sample.
