## SPI control, diagnosis, and reset behavior

Each switch has its own MCU SPI bus: U601 on LPSPI4, U701 on LPSPI5, U801 on LPSPI3. No SCLK, CS, SDI or SDO line is shared (<Ref id="DEC-018" />). The Rev A daisy chain let one failed switch cut off all six outputs; now a bus fault affects two. Settings: SPI mode 1 (CPOL 0, CPHA 1), 8 MHz maximum, 32-bit frames with CRC, and 45 ns minimum CS setup and hold. Read back every protection register, and keep one FLT/WAKE line per device. All channels stay off after reset; LHI is held inactive in hardware.

Each switch's SPI watchdog is on (WD_EN = 1, WD_TO = 400 ms), with CHx_LH_IN set so both channels turn off in LIMP_HOME. If its bus or the firmware stops talking to a switch, that switch turns both outputs off and pulls FLT low. It returns to normal control on the next valid SPI frame (TPS2HCS08-Q1 data sheet SLVSHR0, §8.3.7, version A).

| Function | Implementation | Failure behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Output command | SPI CHx control bits | All channels commanded off after reset |
| Fault summary | 3 × FLT/WAKE to MCU | Interrupt identifies the affected dual device |
| Detailed diagnosis | SPI status and ADC registers | Log channel, raw reading, mode, and timestamp |
| SPI integrity | One bus per switch, CRC | Fault on CRC, timeout, or readback mismatch; the fault is located to one switch |
| Loss of SPI | Per-switch SPI watchdog, 400 ms | That switch's two outputs off (limp-home), FLT low |

## SNS path and 100 mA resolution

Each device’s shared SNS output feeds one MCU ADC channel through a 698 Ω resistor and a low-leakage filter. SPI selects the observed output. The selection state is stored with every sample. No per-output shunt or external monitor is fitted; the shared 0.25 mΩ LM74910H RSENSE and INA228A-Q1 independently monitor the total input rail.

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| MCU ADC LSB | 3.3 V / 4096 | 0.806 mV |
| 100 mA, KSNS1 | 0.1 A / 5,000 × 698 Ω | 13.96 mV · 17.3 counts |
| 10 A, KSNS1 | 10 A / 5,000 × 698 Ω | 1.396 V · 1,732 counts |
| 100 mA, KSNS2 | 0.1 A / 1,400 × 698 Ω | 49.86 mV · 61.9 counts |

<Aside type="tip" title="Resolution, not absolute accuracy">
The nominal count separation supports a 100 mA resolution claim after filtering and calibration. It does not imply ±100 mA absolute accuracy. Baseline KSNS1 ratio error is ±18% at 100 mA, improving to ±5% at 1 A and ±4% at 10 A.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="I²t and low-current mode interact">
KSNS2 improves low-current ratio accuracy, but TI recommends using KSNS2 and 8× ADC scaling only with I²t disabled; altered scaling can lower the effective I²t trip threshold. Rev A keeps KSNS1 while I²t is active and budgets ±18% at 100 mA (<Ref id="DEC-009" />).
</Aside>

## I²t protection profile

- Program nominal current, I²t trip, fixed-delay overcurrent, immediate shutdown, cooldown, and retry from a harness-specific profile.
- Enable I²t only after register readback and verified current-sense operation.
- Keep immediate shutdown active for hard shorts; I²t handles sustained overload energy.
- Unlimited automatic retry is prohibited. Retain source and branch fusing wherever the switch cannot protect upstream conductors.
- Log accumulation, trip source, retries, raw current, supply, and temperature.

## Conduction and thermal budget

At 10 A, 8.9 mΩ produces 0.89 W per channel at 25 °C; 17.8 mΩ hot typical produces 1.78 W. Six outputs therefore dissipate approximately **5.34 W cold** or **10.68 W hot**, before connector, copper, regulator, and switching losses.
