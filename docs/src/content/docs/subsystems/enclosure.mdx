The board mounts component side down under the lid, 2.6 mm thick, with every connector on a fixed enclosure face (<Ref id="DEC-020" />). This page gives the enclosure that follows from it: the outside size, where each panel cutout goes, and where the board sits.

<Aside type="caution" title="Provisional and generated">
No enclosure is chosen yet. The board outline and the wall, gap and floor clearances are working assumptions. Every view and table here is generated at docs build time by `scripts/enclosure_drawing.py` (the handbook's enclosure-drawing skill) from `scripts/enclosure_geometry.py`, which reads the positions from `scripts/place_connectors.py`. Change them there and the next build redraws everything.
</Aside>

## Sources and assumptions

- **Board and connector positions:** `scripts/place_connectors.py`, the same positions as on `node60.kicad_pcb`. Each board edge sits {g.assumptions.edge_gap} mm behind the connector flange face it meets, so the flange, not the board, meets the wall.
- **AT13 cutout:** Amphenol drawing C-AT13-2P-BMXX rev. A2, "recommended panel cutout". The mating axis and screw holes are 13.8 mm from the board.
- **ATM13 cutout:** Amphenol drawing ATM13-2P-BMXX rev. A2. The drawing gives no height above the board. The mating axis (15.0 mm) is measured from the shroud in Amphenol's STEP model, and the cutout is placed with its holes on that axis.
- **SurLok Plus lid cutout:** Amphenol catalog IC-10, p. 13 (5.7 mm). The board-to-lid distance follows from p. 6: the contact face lands on the board's back face, {f(g.assumptions.surlok_depth)} mm from the panel face.
- **Assumed:** {g.wall} mm walls, floor and lid; {g.assumptions.back_gap} mm from the board to the back wall; {g.assumptions.floor_gap} mm between the deepest header ({g.assumptions.deepest_header} mm) and the floor.
