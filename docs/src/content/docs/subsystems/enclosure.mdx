The board mounts component side up, 2.6 mm thick. A copper busbar on top of the board joins each power pair, and the SurLok receptacles sit on the busbars in two low sections of the lid, either side of a raised T that covers the output headers (crossbar) and the T1S pair on the north wall (stem) (<Ref id="DEC-021" />). This page gives the enclosure that follows from it: the outside size, where each panel cutout goes, and where the board sits.

<Aside type="caution" title="Provisional and generated">

## Sources and assumptions

- **Board and connector positions:** `scripts/place_connectors.py`, the same positions as on `node60.kicad_pcb`. Each board edge sits {g.assumptions.edge_gap} mm behind the connector flange face it meets, so the flange, not the board, meets the wall.
- **AT13 cutout:** Amphenol drawing C-AT13-2P-BMXX rev. A2, "recommended panel cutout". The mating axis and screw holes are 13.8 mm from the board.
- **ATM13 cutout:** Amphenol drawing ATM13-2P-BMXX rev. A2. The drawing gives no height above the board. The mating axis (15.0 mm) is measured from the shroud in Amphenol's STEP model, and the cutout is placed with its holes on that axis.
- **SurLok Plus lid cutout:** Amphenol catalog IC-10, p. 13 (5.7 mm). The low-lid height follows from p. 6: the contact face lands on the busbar, {f(g.assumptions.surlok_depth)} mm from the panel face.
- **Busbars:** 1/8" × 1" C110 copper flat bar, the thinnest stock bar that carries the current, so the most of the SurLok stud reaches the REDCUBE thread. The stud length is not published; see DEC-021.
- **Assumed:** {g.wall} mm walls, floor and lid; the board {g.assumptions.standoff} mm off the floor (the REDCUBE bodies hang 3 mm below it); the raised T {g.assumptions.hood_gap} mm clear of the tallest header ({g.assumptions.deepest_header} mm) and reaching {g.assumptions.hood_gap} mm past the header bodies.
