Each output gets a sealed, independently protected path; the aggregate input is treated as a 60 A interconnect problem, plus the battery current passed through to a downstream node.

| Interface | Architecture | Release condition |
|---|---|---|
| Six outputs | Amphenol Sine AT13-2P-BM04, one 2-pin right-angle header per output (<Ref id="DEC-013" />); 10 A each; 14 AWG minimum | Pin 1 = OUTx, pin 2 = local return; contact temperature rise at 10 A proven up to +125 °C ambient; DT06-2S, sockets, wedgelock, wire, and panel seal frozen together |
| Battery input | Two Würth REDCUBE 74660016R M6 terminals with red Amphenol SurLok Plus SLPRATPSR receptacles in the lid, joined by an internal busbar (<Ref id="DEC-016" />, <Ref id="DEC-020" />) | One receptacle feeds the node, the other passes battery to a downstream node; prove the full current path at <Ref id="GATE-HAR-02" /> |
| Power return | Two REDCUBE 74660016R terminals with black SurLok Plus SLPRATPSB receptacles, busbar-joined; all six output returns come back through the node (<Ref id="DEC-013" />, <Ref id="DEC-016" />) | 60 A plus the downstream return; no chassis-only return assumption |
| 10BASE-T1S | Two Amphenol Sine ATM13-2P-BM04: J903 upstream and J912 downstream, in parallel on the pair; sealed 2-pin right-angle PCB receptacles, mate ATM06-2S (<Ref id="DEC-014" />, <Ref id="DEC-017" />) | Vehicle-level protection and termination strategy; verify pin 1 on a sample |
| Complete node cable | Four conductors: T1S twisted pair + battery-direct power + ground, plus an optional battery/ground pass-through to one downstream node | No KL15, ignition, or local hardwire wake input; controller ECU must provide T1S wake (<Ref id="REQ-NET-001" />) |

## Output connectors

Each output has its own **Amphenol Sine AT13-2P-BM04** header: BoardLock AT13, 2 pins at 5.40 mm, right angle, with a panel flange gasket and a potted rear. It mates an AT06-2S plug (Amphenol's DT06-2S equivalent) with size 16 sockets for 14 to 20 AWG. Amphenol rates it 13 A, 250 V, −55 to +125 °C at rated current, and IP67 mated. It seals against the enclosure wall through the same panel cutout as the DT13-2P. See <Ref id="DEC-013" long />.

**Pin assignment:** pin 1 = OUTx, pin 2 = that output's local ground return. Up to 60 A of load return current (six outputs at 10 A) therefore flows back through the node to the power-entry star ground and out through the node's ground conductor. That conductor, its input contacts, and the board's return path must carry 60 A, matched to the battery feed.

<Aside type="caution" title="Confirm pin 1 on the first sample">
Amphenol's drawing labels pins 1 and 2 on the mounting face, and the footprint follows it. Confirm the moulded cavity numbers on the first sample and label them on the harness drawing, so OUTx and ground cannot be swapped at the harness.
</Aside>

## Power entry and pass-through

Four **Würth REDCUBE SMT 74660016R** terminals (M6 internal thread, 85 A max at 20 °C) sit on the board: J901 and J910 on the battery input, J902 and J911 on the return. Amphenol **SurLok Plus** 5.7 mm receptacles in the lid screw into them through the board: red SLPRATPSR on battery, black SLPRATPSB on return, IP67 mated, 3 N·m on the M6 thread. A busbar inside the case joins each pair, so one receptacle of each pair takes the source cable and the other feeds a lower-current downstream node. See <Ref id="DEC-016" long />.

<Aside type="caution" title="The pass-through bypasses Node60's protection">
The downstream feed leaves before the LM74910H ideal diode and breaker and before the INA228 shunt. Size the source fuse and cable for both nodes, and give the downstream node its own protection. The downstream feed is limited to **40 A** for now (upstream total 100 A), with the busbar carrying it; GATE-HAR-02 confirms the limit or raises it toward 60 A. Sealing of the power entry is not decided yet.
</Aside>

## Enclosure and board mounting

The board mounts with its component side facing the enclosure floor and its back face under the lid, and it is **2.6 mm** thick (<Ref id="DEC-020" long />). Every connector is fixed to an enclosure face; the outline (250 × 160 mm) is provisional until the enclosure is chosen.

| Enclosure face | Connectors | On the board |
|---|---|---|
| Front | J904–J909, OUT1–OUT6, J904 on the left seen from outside | Bottom edge in KiCad, J904 rightmost |
| Right | J903, T1S upstream | Left edge in KiCad |
| Left | J912, T1S downstream | Right edge in KiCad |
| Lid | J910 and J901 (battery) ±48 mm from the centre line, 65 mm from the front edge; J911 and J902 (return) ±75 mm, 125 mm from the front edge | REDCUBE 74660016R on the component side, threads opening through the board |

The headers hang below the board, and each board edge sits 0.5 mm behind the flange face it meets. Each SurLok receptacle's contact face lands on the board's back face, so the lid's outer face sits about 19.2 mm above the board. KiCad's top view looks at the component side, so its left and right are mirrored against the enclosure seen from outside.

Each SurLok right-angle plug can leave west, north or east. All 81 combinations of the four plugs clear each other and the receptacle flanges by at least 3 mm, and a plug pointing west or east stays inside the enclosure walls, checked with the catalog envelopes by `scripts/check_surlok_clearance.py`.

**[Open the 3D enclosure mockup](../../mockups/node60-enclosure.html)**: drag to orbit, lift the lid, and turn each plug west, north or east.

<Aside type="caution" title="Header pin tails on a 2.6 mm board">
The AT13 tails are 3.1 ± 0.4 mm and the ATM13 tails 3.05 mm, so they protrude only about 0.1 to 0.5 mm. Confirm the solder joints with Amphenol or on the first assembled sample.
</Aside>

## Fusing

Place the primary fuse at the battery/source end, sized for Node60 plus the downstream node fed through the pass-through. If the 60 A path is split, fuse each feed and prevent back-powering through an undersized path. Final values coordinate wire ampacity, contact derating, load inrush, PCB fault energy, and the smart-switch curve. Use branch fuses wherever the main fuse cannot protect an output conductor or connector fault.

<Aside type="caution" title="Connector freeze condition">
Connector families are architectural selections, not released mating sets. Freeze exact housings, terminals, seals, wire sizes, and keys together; validate temperature rise with all intended adjacent circuits loaded.
</Aside>
