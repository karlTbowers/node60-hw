## Output connectors

Each output has its own **Amphenol Sine AT13-2P-BM04** header: BoardLock AT13, 2 pins at 5.40 mm, right angle, with a panel flange gasket and a potted rear. It mates an AT06-2S plug (Amphenol's DT06-2S equivalent) with size 16 sockets for 14 to 20 AWG. Amphenol rates it 13 A, 250 V, −55 to +125 °C at rated current, and IP67 mated. It seals against the enclosure wall through the same panel cutout as the DT13-2P. See <Ref id="DEC-013" long />.

**Pin assignment:** pin 1 = OUTx, pin 2 = that output's local ground return. Up to 60 A of load return current (six outputs at 10 A) therefore flows back through the node to the power-entry star ground and out through the node's ground conductor. That conductor, its input contacts, and the board's return path must carry 60 A, matched to the battery feed.

<Aside type="caution" title="Confirm pin 1 on the first sample">
Amphenol's drawing labels pins 1 and 2 on the mounting face, and the footprint follows it. Confirm the moulded cavity numbers on the first sample and label them on the harness drawing, so OUTx and ground cannot be swapped at the harness.
</Aside>

## Power entry and pass-through

Four **Würth REDCUBE SMT 74660016R** terminals (M6 internal thread, 85 A max at 20 °C) sit on the board: J901 and J910 on the battery input, J902 and J911 on the return. Amphenol **SurLok Plus** 5.7 mm receptacles in the case wall screw into them: red SLPRATPSR on battery, black SLPRATPSB on return, IP67 mated, 3 N·m on the M6 thread. A busbar inside the case joins each pair, so one side of the case takes the source cables and the other side feeds a lower-current downstream node. See <Ref id="DEC-016" long />.

<Aside type="caution" title="The pass-through bypasses Node60's protection">
The downstream feed leaves before the LM74910H ideal diode and breaker and before the INA228 shunt. Size the source fuse and cable for both nodes, and give the downstream node its own protection. The downstream current limit is set at GATE-HAR-02. Sealing of the power entry is not decided yet.
</Aside>

## Fusing

Place the primary fuse at the battery/source end, sized for Node60 plus the downstream node fed through the pass-through. If the 60 A path is split, fuse each feed and prevent back-powering through an undersized path. Final values coordinate wire ampacity, contact derating, load inrush, PCB fault energy, and the smart-switch curve. Use branch fuses wherever the main fuse cannot protect an output conductor or connector fault.

<Aside type="caution" title="Connector freeze condition">
Connector families are architectural selections, not released mating sets. Freeze exact housings, terminals, seals, wire sizes, and keys together; validate temperature rise with all intended adjacent circuits loaded.
</Aside>
