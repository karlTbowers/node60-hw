## Rev B (in progress)

Changes accepted since Rev A. Rev B is tagged when the user closes it.

- T1S line-interface order changed: the DC block now sits on the connector side of the choke ([DEC-012](../decisions/dec-012/)).
- The output switches moved from one LPSPI4 daisy chain to one LPSPI bus per switch: LPSPI4, LPSPI5, LPSPI3 ([DEC-018](../decisions/dec-018/)). Each switch's SPI watchdog turns its outputs off if SPI stops. GATE-MCU-01 was re-run for the eight added balls and passed (2026-10-30).
- Log-flash second source changed from W25Q32JVSSSQ (208-mil SOIC, does not fit the footprint) to the 150-mil W25Q32JVSNSM ([DEC-011](../decisions/dec-011/)).
- Zephyr recorded as the core RTOS, with its driver map and pin-plan rules ([DEC-019](../decisions/dec-019/)).
- The board mounts component side down under the lid, 2.6 mm thick, with every connector on a fixed enclosure face and all four power receptacles in the lid ([DEC-020](../decisions/dec-020/)).
- Board component side up again, busbars under the SurLok receptacles, a lid with a raised T, and the T1S connectors on the north wall ([DEC-021](../decisions/dec-021/), supersedes DEC-020).
- Eight candidate enclosure designs kept side by side on the [Enclosure options](../subsystems/enclosure/) pages, each with a generated drawing, a 3D mockup and a plug check.
- Enclosure version 7 chosen: a lid with a raised front band over the outputs and a separate hood over the T1S pair, staggered receptacles and a removable north panel; DEC-021's board, terminals and busbars stay ([DEC-022](../decisions/dec-022/), supersedes DEC-021).
- The board is 2.0 mm thick instead of 2.6 mm (enclosure version 10): the busbars rest on the REDCUBE spigots, which stand 0.6 mm proud, and the raised lid sections drop 0.6 mm ([DEC-023](../decisions/dec-023/), supersedes DEC-022).
- The board is 230 × 110 mm (enclosure version 11, version 7 made as small as it goes, on 2.0 mm), after a placement trial fitted every part, nearly all on the bottom side ([DEC-024](../decisions/dec-024/), supersedes DEC-023).
- The busbars are 1/8" × 3/4" instead of 1/8" × 1" (enclosure version 15): the same top-side area comes free as with version 14's 3/16" × 3/4" bars, and the SurLok stud still engages the REDCUBE's whole thread ([DEC-025](../decisions/dec-025/), supersedes DEC-024).
- Both busbars are schematic and BOM parts: BB901 a net tie between J910's VBAT_IN and J901's new net VBAT_IN_DOWNSTREAM, with no board copper between the two terminals, and BB902 the same part with both pins on GND ([DEC-016](../decisions/dec-016/) rev. B).
- The 1.5 V core buck U303 is a TPS628502-Q1 fed from 3V3_MAIN, at a fixed 2.25 MHz with spread spectrum and forced PWM, instead of a second LMQ66430-Q1 on the battery, which folded back into the AM band; VBAT_FILT now feeds only the 3.3 V buck ([DEC-026](../decisions/dec-026/)).
- Regulator-branch EMI filter parts chosen (2026-11-03): L201 Würth 74438356022HT 2.2 µH, C207 Nichicon GYF1H470MCQ1GS 47 µF 50 V hybrid polymer on the top side, and C217 the same part not fitted, for bench tuning ([Power entry & regulated rails](../subsystems/power/)).
- The shared input shunt R201 is the 4-terminal 0.3 mΩ Isabellenhütte BVR-Z-R0003-1.0 instead of the 2-terminal 0.25 mΩ CSS2725FTL250, with its sense terminals on their own nets (`RS_SENSE_P`, `RS_SENSE_N`). R(ILIM) becomes 26.7 kΩ (74.8 A breaker) and RISCP 383 Ω, so the fast trip keeps its 80 A (71.4–92 A) window; the INA228's SHUNT_CAL becomes 4096 with a 260.4167 µA current LSB (decision D14-1, 2026-11-03; [DEC-001](../decisions/dec-001/) and [DEC-002](../decisions/dec-002/) rev. B).
- A second T1S ESD pair, D503/D504, sits at J912's pins, so each T1S connector has its own clamps; the node's parts now total 20.5 pF of the OPEN Alliance CMDI ≤ 25 pF budget at data-sheet maxima (decision D16-2, 2026-11-03; [DEC-012](../decisions/dec-012/) rev. C).
- Both battery terminals are VBAT_IN: either may take the source or feed the downstream node, since the harness decides which (user, 2026-11-04). The power entry is mirrored about the midpoint between J910 and J901, each terminal feeding its own ideal-diode FET; the ideal-diode FETs sit beside the LM74910H so DGATE is about 23 mm, as TI's layout guidelines require a short trace. The 60 A chain measures 0.76 mΩ from the copper (was 3.56 mΩ), and the two sides are within 2.5 % (placement decisions D20 A and D21 C; [DEC-016](../decisions/dec-016/) rev. C).
- The PCB is 6 layers, 2.0 mm, made by JLCPCB on its stackup JLC062012-1080 (1 oz outer, 2 oz inner copper), after instant quotes in 6 and 8 layers at 1.6 and 2.0 mm ([DEC-027](../decisions/dec-027/)). Outer copper weight and the via option stay open.

## Rev A

The first complete architecture. Earlier drafts were incomplete and are not numbered revisions. Rev A replaced these draft choices:

- LM74800QDRRRQ1 and its standalone 0.5 mΩ shunt replaced by LM74910HQRGERQ1 with a shared 0.25 mΩ RSENSE, adding input-rail overcurrent protection ([DEC-001](../decisions/dec-001/)), with INA228A-Q1 as total-current monitor ([DEC-002](../decisions/dec-002/)).
- The FS26 SBC removed in favour of a discrete INH-gated power tree with a nanopower VDDAU LDO ([DEC-004](../decisions/dec-004/)).
- IS25LP032D-JNLA3 diagnostic flash added ([DEC-011](../decisions/dec-011/)), with T1S-only wake and controller-commanded shutdown ([DEC-007](../decisions/dec-007/)).

Rev A was first written as a single-file HTML document (labelled Rev C at the time) and ported to this site on 2026-10-21 (handbook chapter 08). That file is no longer published; it remains in the repository's git history.
