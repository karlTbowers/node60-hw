The baseline is ready for schematic capture, not production release. These tests convert architectural assumptions into evidence.

## Bring-up sequence

1. **Power-entry coupon:** reverse battery, OV disconnect, TVS clamp, cold crank, hot plug, parked leakage, the recalculated 0.6 V OV/UV thresholds, and BATT_MON wake-state accuracy without compromising the parked-current budget.
2. **Input protection:** prove the 75 A OCP breaker threshold, default 80 A fast SCP trip, FLT pre-trip signaling, TMR ride-through versus inrush, and SLEEP-mode auto-retry behavior.
3. **Shared-RSENSE path:** verify separate star Kelvin routing, INA228A-Q1 I²C communication, SHUNT_CAL for 0.25 mΩ, RSENSE temperature at 60 A continuous, 0–60 A accuracy across temperature, and total-vs-sum cross-check. Confirm buck/MCU rails originate at VOUT after Q2.
4. **Temperature network:** verify shared-bus TMP175 communication and optional ALERT, inspect all four NTC placements, spot-check conversion accuracy against a calibrated reference, and test derating/logging thresholds including the TPS2HCS08 I²t telemetry blackout.
5. **Reset supervision:** inject 3V3_MAIN undervoltage and overvoltage events, scope TPS3702-to-RESET_b timing, spot-check thresholds across temperature, and fault-inject SWT and CMU reset paths.
6. **Bus-wake power cycle:** measure parked current, T1S wake detection, INH assertion, rail ramp plus PHY initialization within 15 ms (OPEN Alliance limit), EN-chain order, whole-board inrush, cold boot, IRQ_N ready, PHY re-initialization, PLCA restore, and application readiness within the accepted ≈1 s budget.
7. **Commanded shutdown:** inject the controller’s application-level go-to-sleep message; prove all outputs turn off, pending pages finish programming, PHY sleep is commanded only afterward, INH releases after tSLPINHDLY, and no bus-idle timeout can trigger shutdown.
8. **Flash endurance and recovery:** worst-case ten-page pre-sleep flush, ahead-erase, FCB wrap, erase suspend/resume, CRC recovery after interrupted power, log readback over T1S, and NVS counter persistence.
9. **Regulator board:** startup, shutdown, load transient, current limit, EN-pin sequencing, and thermal margin across temperature.
10. **MCU-only:** package map, clocks, reset, SWD, ADC reference, both LPSPI instances, and Zephyr baseline.
11. **One-switch fixture:** daisy-chain framing and CRC, reset-off, SNS mux, FLT/WAKE, output control, and register readback.
12. **Current sweep:** all channels 0–10 A; every 100 mA point from 0–1 A; noise, settling, monotonicity, error, supply, and temperature.
13. **I²t fixture:** correlate each protection setting against current, temperature, supply, wire, connector, inrush, and short impedance.
14. **Dual-channel thermal:** both channels at 10 A at worst ambient in the sealed enclosure—release-blocking qualification.
15. **Six-channel assembly:** 60 A matrix, fuse coordination, harness faults, ground offsets, switching during network traffic, EMC, and enclosure thermal.
